Welcome to IBM MaaS360

I am very pleased to have you on this team. Your contributions will be valued.

This is a working list to get you ready to make an impact. Much of this is self-paced and we will add and modify it along the way. During the next few weeks, you will also attend several one-on-one meetings with me, other managers and peers to learn all about this business.

Enjoy the start of a long and rewarding career at IBM MaaS360.

Your onboarding roadmap

Six weeks from foundation to certification. Self-paced, but this is the shape of it.

Weeks 1–2

Foundation and introduction

  • Territory review
  • Business partner introductions
  • Team introductions
  • ISC – best practices
  • Sign new IPL (later in the month)
  • Develop 30-day plan

Prospecting strategy and tactics

  • Inbound
  • Outbound
  • Organic growth
Weeks 3–4

Deep dive and practice

  • Daily, 30 min touch base with Mike
  • ISC/OI review
  • Schedule 1:1's with each team member for best practices
  • Review of MaaS360, portal walk through
  • Begin shadow calls – as many as possible, target 10 calls over the next two weeks
  • Connect with your peers directly
  • Review 30-day plan

Key dashboards and tools

  • Forecast (Seller) Dashboard
  • Seller-Pipeline Dashboard
  • Team Dashboards
  • SSABL
  • FastPass
  • Engage Support
  • CS360
  • Customer Lists

Sales motions

  • Sales-Discovery-Demo
  • Qualify thru Close
  • Pricing/Quoting
  • Closing
  • Digital Enablers
Weeks 5–6

Mastery and certification

  • Practice MaaS360 demo
  • Stand and delivers
  • Finish any security learning academy and MaaS360 L1/L2/L3
  • Review of MaaS360, portal walk through and workflows
  • Shadow more calls
  • Final practice stand and deliver
