Your Onboarding Roadmap

Six weeks from foundation to certification. Self-paced, but this is the shape of it.

Weeks 1–2

Foundation & Introduction

  • Territory Review
  • Business Partner Introductions
  • Team Introductions
  • ISC – Best Practices
  • Sign new IPL (later in the month)
  • Develop 30-day plan

Prospecting Strategy and Tactics

  • Inbound
  • Outbound
  • Organic Growth
Weeks 3–4

Deep Dive & Practice

  • Daily, 30 min touch base with Mike
  • ISC/OI review
  • Schedule 1:1's with each team member for best practices
  • Review of MaaS360, portal walk through
  • Begin Shadow Calls – as many as possible, target 10 calls over the next two weeks
  • Connect with your peers directly
  • Review 30-day plan

Key Dashboards & Tools

  • Forecast (Seller) Dashboard
  • Seller-Pipeline Dashboard
  • Team Dashboards
  • SSABL
  • FastPass
  • Engage Support
  • CS360
  • Customer Lists

Sales Motions

  • Sales-Discovery-Demo
  • Qualify thru Close
  • Pricing/Quoting
  • Closing
  • Digital Enablers
Weeks 5–6

Mastery & Certification

  • Practice MaaS360 Demo
  • Stand and delivers
  • Finish any security learning academy and MaaS360 L1/L2/L3
  • Review of MaaS360, portal walk through and workflows
  • Shadow more calls
  • Final Practice stand and deliver
